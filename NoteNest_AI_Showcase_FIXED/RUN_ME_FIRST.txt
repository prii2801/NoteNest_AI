NOTENEST AI — FIXED SHOWCASE VERSION

IMPORTANT BEFORE YOU START
This version fixes:
1. duplicate note creation,
2. the editor/attachment preview not clearing after Save,
3. AI not reading PDFs, Word documents and images.

----------------------------------------
A. IF YOU WANT TO KEEP YOUR EXISTING DATA
----------------------------------------

Your notes are stored in MongoDB, so use the SAME MONGO_URI.

Your uploaded files are stored physically inside the old project's:
backend\uploads

If you want old uploaded PDFs/images to keep working:
1. Open your OLD project.
2. Copy everything inside:
   backend\uploads
3. Paste those files into:
   NoteNest_AI_Showcase_FIXED\backend\uploads

Also copy your OLD backend\.env into the new backend folder.
